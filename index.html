From discovery to decision

How to use your ACBuy spreadsheet shortlist

A good shortlist makes missing information visible before money changes hands.

01 / DISCOVER

Identify the exact item

Search the catalog, open the current listing and record the seller, source URL, color, size and model. Similar names can describe different products or variants.

02 / COMPARE

Resolve fit and quality questions

Compare measurements with something you own. Ask whether inspection photos are available for your order. Reference QC photos may show another batch or variant.

03 / CONFIRM

Check support and the total

Confirm that your selected agent can buy the exact listing. Review payment terms, return limits, storage deadlines and a destination-specific shipping quote.

Price is only the beginning

Build a more complete order budget.

Use your own quotes in USD. Blank fields are treated as zero for arithmetic only; a blank charge is still unknown until confirmed.

  • Shoes: confirm the sizing system, width and relevant measurements.
  • Clothing: compare chest, length, waist or inseam in consistent units.
  • QC photos: check the selected variant, visible condition and any missing views.
  • Shipping: ask about billable weight, packaging, exclusions and quote validity.

Keep the useful evidence

What to save beside every product link

Source & variant

Save the original marketplace URL when available, seller name, model reference, size, color and date checked. An agent-specific link may be less useful if platform support changes.

Measurements & observations

Separate seller claims from your own observations. Add measurement units and concrete questions such as “confirm sleeve length” rather than a vague quality score.

Quote & order terms

Keep the item currency, exchange-rate quote, service fees and shipping estimate. Record return conditions and the source of each answer so you can revisit the decision.

A little more clarity

ACBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is an ACBuy spreadsheet?

An ACBuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize finds, source links, prices and buying notes. This independent page provides a dated CAFANS catalog sample rather than an official ACBuy inventory feed.

Where do the two shopping buttons go?

Both “ACBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” open https://cafans.com. They open the catalog rather than placing an order or completing payment.

Are the displayed prices the final delivered cost?

No. Displayed USD amounts are estimates observed on CAFANS on November 19, 2026. Confirm current item prices, fees, delivery charges and any applicable import charges before paying.

Can every listed item be ordered through ACBuy?

This page does not confirm current ACBuy purchasing support. Confirm that your chosen agent accepts the exact source listing and destination before placing an order.

Are these products QC verified?

No independent product inspection was performed for this page. Catalog inclusion is a discovery reference. Request inspection images tied to your specific order and review their limits before authorizing shipment.

Does this page update automatically?

No. The table is a static import of 12 CAFANS homepage entries checked on November 19, 2026. Search and category filters work locally on this sample. Open CAFANS for current listings and prices.

How do I handle an unavailable or changed listing?

Mark the entry unavailable and keep its original source URL and variant notes for reference. Check any replacement independently; a similar photograph or title does not establish that it is the same product.
